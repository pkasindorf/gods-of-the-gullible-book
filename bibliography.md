# Bibliography — Gods of the Gullible

*Created: 2026-10-19*

> *In this file, "I," "me," and "my" mean Peter, the author. "The LLM" means the AI assistant (any large language model).*

The single source of truth for every source the book cites or may cite. Other files point here instead of repeating citation details.

## How to use this file

- Every quotation, statistic, research finding, and borrowed idea needs an entry here before it goes into a draft.
- **Status** values:
  - **Unverified**: the details come from a brain note or the LLM's memory. Nobody has checked them against the actual book.
  - **Verified**: checked against the actual edition. Publisher, year, edition, and page are filled in.
  - **Lead**: a web article, video, or AI summary. It points toward a source but is not evidence.
- Never invent missing details. Leave the field as "—" until it is checked.
- Quotations in the brain notes (for example the blockquotes in `Trilectic-god.md` and `high-controlled-research-1.md`) must be checked word for word against the original before use. They may be paraphrases or AI wording.
