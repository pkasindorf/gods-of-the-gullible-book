## Interview Questions

These are open questions about Peter's personal story and experience. Answers will shape Chapter 2 (My Personal Story) and the practical tools sections.

### Personal Story — The Chronological Arc

1. **After the Santa realization** — You knew the truth at ten and kept performing belief. What happened next? Did that split close, or did it widen? When did it surface again?

2. **The religious family** — What did Force 2 look like from the inside growing up? What were the specific beliefs, rules, or expectations that were handed to you without examination?

3. **Entering a high-control group** — Walk me through the moment or period when you joined your first high-control environment (EST / The Forum, Direct Centering, Nexus, or the 12-step program). What were you looking for? What did Force 2 look like inside that group?

4. **The first crack** — When did you first notice your Inner Council operating against the Force 2 you had been given? Was it a moment, a question, a person, a book?

5. **"Gullible no more"** — Was the arrival at that destination a single moment or a gradual shift? What does it feel like now compared to before?

### The Inner Council in Practice

6. **A real decision** — Describe a decision you made recently where you consciously used your Inner Council. What were Force 1 and Force 2 pulling toward? What did the Thirdspace decide?

7. **Your own moral compass** — Outside of religion and the 12 steps, how do you describe the values you actually operate from? Where did they come from?

### The Book's Argument

8. **The reader you're writing for** — Who is the specific person you most want to reach? Someone still inside a high-control environment, someone who just left, or someone who has been out for years and still doesn't know why they feel the way they do?

9. **The one tool** — Of all the practical tools sketched in the framework, which one do you use most in your own life right now?
