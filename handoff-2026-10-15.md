### Research fixes: "Force 2 colonization" (Q1, Q2)

**The core problem.** The trilectical research notes define gullibility *as* Force 2 colonization of the Thirdspace, and as something that "fills the space where the Inner Council has not been built." My current model says Force 3 always exists and always rules, and gullibility is Force 3 *corrupted* by unsubstantiated facts.

**The proposed fix.** Keep "Force 2 colonization," but demote it from *what gullibility is* to *one route by which gullibility gets in*. Colonization is the delivery. Gullibility is the corruption that results once Force 3 commits the claims.
