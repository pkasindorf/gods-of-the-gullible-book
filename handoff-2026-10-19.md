## What was decided this session

### The model (now in `EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md` → The Inner Council)

- **Force 3 = Thirdspace.** Firstspace = Force 1, Secondspace = Force 2. The two sets of names are synonyms for the three parts of trilectical thinking. Which set readers see is still an open question.
- **Gullibility:** the Inner Council accepts claims without demanding evidence. Firstspace and Secondspace deliberate, and the Thirdspace signs off without asking for proof.
- **The Thirdspace is always the final decision-maker.** It is never missing and is never "unbuilt." Like a child, it is developed or underdeveloped, mature or immature. An underdeveloped Thirdspace still decides, and decides poorly.
- **Only external forces can corrupt the council.** Force 1 (biology) is a council member, so it cannot be a source of corruption.
- **Force 2** holds beliefs the Thirdspace already approved, many inherited. That creates bias toward culture, dogma, and rule-based right and wrong. As the Thirdspace matures, it may clash with what Force 2 holds. That clash is cognitive dissonance.
- **"Gullible no more"** = a mature Thirdspace that demands proof before it signs off on a claim.
- **"Force 2 colonization" is retired.**

### Leads (not settled)

- **Colonization:** corruption of the council by self-serving external forces. I like it but don't know where it fits yet. It did not originate with me. It first appears, unsourced, in the LLM-written research notes. Possible prior credit (unverified): Habermas, Lefebvre.
- **The illusion of God may colonize the Thirdspace.** This conflicts with "God is found in Force 3." It is logged as an open question in `EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md`.
